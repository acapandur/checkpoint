Explainer

What is a CheckPoint?

A CheckPoint is a visible, low-threshold sexual-health entry point where people can ask, test, get oriented and move toward care without shame.

Why the idea matters

Many people do not need a dramatic campaign. They need a clear place to start: confidential testing, counseling, prevention information, referral and calm language. A CheckPoint network turns that first step into a public service instead of a private maze.

Low threshold

Entry should be easy to understand, easy to find and not dependent on knowing the right insider language.

Confidential

Privacy is not a side issue. It is part of access, especially where stigma makes people delay care.

Connected

Testing, PrEP, PEP, counseling, vaccination, treatment referral and mobile access should not feel like separate puzzles.

Campaign position

The game does not claim that one model solves every problem. It shows why a visible national network is easier to trust than isolated, hard-to-find routes.
