PrEP

Pre-exposure prophylaxis: prevention before possible HIV exposure. Medicine that HIV-negative people can take so an exposure is far less likely to become an infection. It runs through a prescription, an HIV test first and regular check-ups, and it targets HIV specifically — condoms still matter for other infections. In the story, this is Ivan's night: asking about it is responsibility, not confession. Predekspozicijska profilaksa: prevencija prije moguće izloženosti HIV-u. Lijek koji HIV-negativne osobe mogu uzimati kako izloženost mnogo teže postane infekcija. Ide uz recept, prethodni HIV test i redovite kontrole te cilja specifično HIV – kondomi su i dalje važni za druge infekcije. U priči je to Ivanova noć: pitati o tome odgovornost je, a ne priznanje.

PEP

Post-exposure prophylaxis: the urgent route after a possible HIV exposure. PEP can reduce the chance of HIV infection when started in time after professional assessment and taken as prescribed. It is not guaranteed. PEP is time-sensitive. Seek professional help as soon as possible, ideally within 24 hours and no later than 72 hours after possible exposure. In the story, this is Ema's night: hours are the currency. Postekspozicijska profilaksa: hitna ruta nakon moguće izloženosti HIV-u. PEP može smanjiti mogućnost infekcije HIV-om ako se započne na vrijeme nakon stručne procjene i uzima prema uputama. Nije jamstvo. PEP je vremenski osjetljiv. Potražite stručnu pomoć što prije, idealno unutar 24 sata, a najkasnije unutar 72 sata nakon mogućeg izlaganja. U priči je to Emina noć: sati su valuta.
