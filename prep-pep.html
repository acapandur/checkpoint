PrEP and PEP

Prevention and urgency should not be hard to find.

The campaign keeps PrEP and PEP language simple and cautious: ask qualified professionals, route urgent concerns quickly and make prevention easier to discuss.

Medical safety note

This prototype does not decide whether PrEP or PEP is right for anyone. It does not replace qualified medical advice. Public launch copy should be reviewed by medical experts and localized to Croatian services.

PrEP

PrEP is prevention before possible HIV exposure. The campaign frames it as a normal prevention question that people should be able to ask without stigma.

PEP

PEP is an urgent post-exposure route. Official sources state it should be started as soon as possible and no later than 72 hours after a possible exposure.

Clear navigation

The system problem is not only medical knowledge. It is whether people know where, when and how to reach qualified care.

How the game uses this

When a signal is time-sensitive, the strongest route is not a generic fact card. The game rewards clear urgent routing, calm language and a professional care path. When a signal is about prevention, the game rewards counseling and PrEP pathway clarity.
