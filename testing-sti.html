Testing

Testing is normal planning, not a confession.

A connected network makes testing easier to find, easier to understand and easier to approach privately.

What blocks testing

People can delay testing because they do not know where to go, what to ask for, whether it is confidential, what happens next or whether they will be judged. The game turns those barriers into visible route problems.

Panel clarity

Testing routes should explain that sexual health may involve more than one infection or one test.

Reminders

Clear follow-up and repeat testing guidance should be part of care, not left to memory and anxiety.

Referral

Testing only works as a system when people can move toward treatment, prevention and counseling.

No personal stories in the MVP

This website avoids forms and personal health input. Feedback should be handled through a campaign contact channel with a clear warning not to send private medical details.
