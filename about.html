About

A campaign should be judged by its public-health argument.

The blueprint calls for public-facing anonymity so the campaign foregrounds source review, community support and the network demand.

Public anonymity statement

This campaign is published by a public-health civic initiative. The creator is not foregrounded because the campaign should be judged by its public-health argument, source review and community support, not by one person's identity.

Clean metadata

Public files should avoid personal names, local paths, hidden comments and private author metadata.

Campaign accounts

Public hosting, social and contact channels should use initiative identity and private commit e-mail settings.

No sensitive forms

The MVP avoids collecting personal stories or medical details through forms.

Before launch

Review Git commit identity, PDF metadata, social account recovery settings, image metadata, contact e-mail headers and petition-platform administrator visibility.
