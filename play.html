Privacy policy

Safety and privacy

No personal details belong in this game.

This is an educational prototype, not medical advice or a diagnostic tool. If someone may be in danger or needs urgent medical help, they should contact emergency services, a qualified health professional or a trusted local support service.

The game uses fictional signal cards and does not ask for sexual-health details, names, contact information or location.
