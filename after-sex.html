Post-sex uncertaintyNesigurnost nakon seksa

Panic is loud. Routes are quiet — and they work. Panika je glasna. Rute su tihe – i djeluju.

A broken condom or a night full of question marks is a health situation, not a moral one. This page collects what calm generally looks like — and points to the professionals who handle the rest. Puknuti kondom ili noć puna upitnika zdravstvena je situacija, a ne moralna. Ova stranica prikuplja kako mir općenito izgleda – i upućuje na stručnjake koji rješavaju ostalo.

If something urgent is happeningAko se događa nešto hitno

If anyone may be in danger, was pressured or hurt, or needs urgent medical help, contact emergency services or a qualified health professional now. This website is educational and cannot assess an individual situation. Ako je netko možda u opasnosti, bio prisiljen ili ozlijeđen, ili treba hitnu medicinsku pomoć, odmah se obrati hitnoj službi ili kvalificiranom zdravstvenom stručnjaku. Ova je stranica edukativna i ne može procijeniti pojedinačnu situaciju.

What Ema's night teachesŠto uči Emina noć

In episode one of the story, a condom breaks at 2:47 a.m. Three things carry Ema through the night, and they generalize well: a person to say it to out loud, one reliable source instead of eleven arguing tabs, and the knowledge that some options are time-sensitive — which turns vague dread into a concrete plan. U prvoj epizodi priče kondom puca u 2:47 ujutro. Time-sensitive: possible HIV exposureVremenski osjetljivo: moguća izloženost HIV-u

PEP — post-exposure prophylaxis — is medicine that can prevent HIV after a possible exposure. Official guidance: start as soon as possible, no later than 72 hours. The route runs through emergency care and infectious-disease clinics. Details on the PrEP & PEP page. PEP – postekspozicijska profilaksa – lijek je koji može spriječiti HIV nakon moguće izloženosti. Službena uputa: početi što prije, najkasnije unutar 72 sata. Ruta ide preko hitne službe i klinika za infektivne bolesti. Detalji su na stranici PrEP i PEP.

Separate question: possible pregnancyZasebno pitanje: moguća trudnoća

If pregnancy is a concern, emergency contraception is its own time-sensitive topic — raise it with a pharmacist or doctor alongside anything else. One clear question at a time is a strategy, not a weakness. Ako postoji mogućnost trudnoće, hitna kontracepcija zasebna je vremenski osjetljiva tema – spomeni je ljekarniku ili liječniku uz sve ostalo. Jedno jasno pitanje po jedno strategija je, a ne slabost.

And when nothing is urgentA kad ništa nije hitno

Most post-sex uncertainty is not an emergency — it is a testing question with a calendar attached. Some infections take time to show on tests, so the useful move is a counseling conversation that times things properly, rather than a 3 a.m. verdict from a search engine. That conversation is exactly what community CheckPoints are built for. Većina nesigurnosti nakon seksa nije hitan slučaj – nego pitanje o testiranju s kalendarom u prilogu. Neke se infekcije na testovima pokažu tek nakon nekog vremena, pa je koristan potez savjetodavni razgovor koji stvari pravilno tempira, a ne presuda tražilice u tri ujutro. Upravo za takav razgovor postoje CheckPointi u zajednici.
