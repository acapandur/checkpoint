Panic is loud. Routes are quiet — and they work. Panika je glasna. Rute su tihe – i djeluju.

A broken condom or a night full of question marks is a health situation, not a moral one. This page collects what calm generally looks like — and points to the professionals who handle the rest. Puknuti kondom ili noć puna upitnika zdravstvena je situacija, a ne moralna. Ova stranica prikuplja kako mir općenito izgleda – i upućuje na stručnjake koji rješavaju ostalo.

What Ema's night teachesŠto uči Emina noć

In episode one of the story, a condom breaks at 2:47 a.m. Three things carry Ema through the night, and they generalize well: a person to say it to out loud, one reliable source instead of eleven arguing tabs, and the knowledge that some options are time-sensitive — which turns vague dread into a concrete plan. U prvoj epizodi priče kondom puca u 2:47 ujutro. Tri stvari nose Emu kroz noć i dobro se poopćavaju: osoba kojoj to može izgovoriti naglas, jedan pouzdan izvor umjesto jedanaest posvađanih kartica i saznanje da su neke opcije vremenski osjetljive – što maglovitu strepnju pretvara u konkretan plan.

Time-sensitive: possible HIV exposureVremenski osjetljivo: moguća izloženost HIV-u

PEP can reduce the chance of HIV infection when started in time after professional assessment and taken as prescribed. It is not guaranteed. PEP is time-sensitive. Seek professional help as soon as possible, ideally within 24 hours and no later than 72 hours after possible exposure. Details on the PrEP & PEP page. PEP može smanjiti mogućnost infekcije HIV-om ako se započne na vrijeme nakon stručne procjene i uzima prema uputama. Nije jamstvo. PEP je vremenski osjetljiv. Potražite stručnu pomoć što prije, idealno unutar 24 sata, a najkasnije unutar 72 sata nakon mogućeg izlaganja. Detalji su na stranici PrEP i PEP.

Separate question: possible pregnancyZasebno pitanje: moguća trudnoća

If pregnancy is a concern, emergency contraception is its own time-sensitive topic — raise it with a pharmacist or doctor alongside anything else. One clear question at a time is a strategy, not a weakness. Ako postoji mogućnost trudnoće, hitna kontracepcija zasebna je vremenski osjetljiva tema – spomeni je ljekarniku ili liječniku uz sve ostalo. Jedno jasno pitanje po jedno strategija je, a ne slabost.

And when nothing is urgentA kad ništa nije hitno

Most post-sex uncertainty is not an emergency — it is a testing question with a calendar attached. Some infections take time to show on tests, so the useful move is a counseling conversation that times things properly, rather than a 3 a.m. verdict from a search engine. That is the kind of low-threshold conversation community services such as CheckPoint Zagreb are meant to support. Većina nesigurnosti nakon seksa nije hitan slučaj – nego pitanje o testiranju s kalendarom u prilogu. Neke se infekcije na testovima pokažu tek nakon nekog vremena, pa je koristan potez savjetodavni razgovor koji stvari pravilno tempira, a ne presuda tražilice u tri ujutro. To je vrsta razgovora niskog praga koju usluge u zajednici poput CheckPointa Zagreb trebaju podržati.
