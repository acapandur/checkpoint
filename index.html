The scandal is not sex. It is silence, stigma and unclear access. Skandal nije seks. Skandal su šutnja, stigma i nejasan pristup.

The campaign runs in three deliberate acts, in this order — because public support is strongest when it follows understanding. Kampanja se odvija u tri promišljena čina, ovim redom – jer je javna podrška najjača kad dolazi nakon razumijevanja.

Act I

The websiteWeb-stranica

Readable, searchable pages in Croatian and English — so people can find official sources and plain-language checkpoints for testing, prevention, privacy and urgent questions. Čitljive stranice na hrvatskom i engleskom, vidljive tražilicama – da ljudi mogu pronaći službene izvore i jasne točke provjere za testiranje, prevenciju, privatnost i hitna pitanja.

◆
Act II

The storyPriča

Three Nights follows Ema, Ivan, Petra and Sara through checkpoints real people face: urgent uncertainty, planned prevention, testing, privacy and distance. Tri noći prate Emu, Ivana, Petru i Saru kroz točke provjere s kojima se stvarni ljudi susreću: hitnu nesigurnost, planiranu prevenciju, testiranje, privatnost i udaljenost.

◆
Act III

The petitionPeticija

Each finished night points to one access demand. The petition is not active yet; for now, the Night Ledger points to the access demands and launch-readiness status. Svaka završena noć vodi prema jednom zahtjevu za pristup. Peticija još nije aktivna; za sada Noćna bilanca vodi na zahtjeve za pristup i status spremnosti za pokretanje.

◆

A short story about a real system gap. Kratka priča o stvarnoj rupi u sustavu.

Every character and event in the story is fictional, and the interactive story never asks for personal details. What is real is the map underneath: where the checkpoints are clear, and where they simply stop. Svaki lik i događaj u priči izmišljen je, a interaktivna priča nikada ne traži osobne podatke. Stvarna je jedino karta ispod svega: gdje su točke provjere jasne, a gdje jednostavno prestaju.

The story ends by translating three nights into access demands: a clear urgent checkpoint, clearer prevention checkpoints, and testing/privacy checkpoints within real reach of every region. Priča završava tako da tri noći prevodi u zahtjeve za pristup: jasnu hitnu točku provjere, jasnije točke provjere za prevenciju i točke provjere za testiranje/privatnost stvarno nadohvat svakoj regiji.
