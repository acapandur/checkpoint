A campaign hub and an interactive night storyKampanjski hub i interaktivna noćna priča

After sex, people need clear paths — not shame. Nakon seksa ljudima trebaju jasni putevi – ne sram.

Play Three Nights, a short episodic story set in a nocturnal Croatia, and see why the country needs a connected CheckPoint network for testing, PrEP, PEP and counseling without stigma. Zaigraj Tri noći, kratku epizodnu priču smještenu u noćnu Hrvatsku, i saznaj zašto zemlji treba povezana mreža CheckPointa za testiranje, PrEP, PEP i savjetovanje bez stigme.

Play the storyIgraj priču Read the demandsPročitaj zahtjeve

The scandal is not sex. It is silence, stigma and unclear access. Skandal nije seks. Skandal su šutnja, stigma i nejasan pristup.

The campaign runs in three deliberate acts, in this order — because a signature is worth most when it follows understanding. Kampanja se odvija u tri promišljena čina, ovim redom – jer potpis najviše vrijedi kad dolazi nakon razumijevanja.

Act I

The websiteWeb-stranica

Readable, searchable pages in Croatian and English — so the facts about testing, PrEP and PEP can be found by anyone, at any hour, through search, partners and press. Čitljive stranice na hrvatskom i engleskom, vidljive tražilicama – da činjenice o testiranju, PrEP-u i PEP-u svatko može pronaći, u bilo koje doba, preko pretrage, partnera i medija.

◆
Act II

The storyPriča

Three Nights follows Ema, Ivan, Petra and Sara through choices real people face — a broken condom at 2:47, a question about PrEP, a test that lives three hours away. Tri noći prate Emu, Ivana, Petru i Saru kroz odluke s kojima se stvarni ljudi susreću – puknuti kondom u 2:47, pitanje o PrEP-u, test udaljen tri sata vožnje.

◆
Act III

The petitionPeticija

Each finished night unlocks one concrete demand. After the third, the Night Ledger hands you the pen — a petition for a connected CheckPoint network. Svaka završena noć otključava jedan konkretan zahtjev. Nakon treće, Noćna bilanca predaje ti pero – peticiju za povezanu mrežu CheckPointa.

◆

A short story about a real system gap. Kratka priča o stvarnoj rupi u sustavu.

Every character and event in the story is fictional, and the game never asks for personal details. What is real is the map underneath: where the routes are clear, and where they simply stop. Svaki lik i događaj u priči izmišljen je, a igra nikada ne traži osobne podatke. Stvarna je jedino karta ispod svega: gdje su rute jasne, a gdje jednostavno prestaju.

Night LedgerNoćna bilanca

A petition should follow understanding, not pressure. Peticija treba slijediti razumijevanje, a ne pritisak.

The story ends by translating three nights into campaign demands: a round-the-clock urgent PEP route, stigma-free PrEP counseling, and a CheckPoint within reach of every region — with confidential care and sustainable public funding. Priča završava tako da tri noći prevodi u zahtjeve kampanje: urgentnu PEP rutu dostupnu 0–24, PrEP savjetovanje bez stigme i CheckPoint dostupan u svakoj regiji – uz povjerljivu skrb i održivo javno financiranje.
