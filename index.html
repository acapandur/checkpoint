A campaign hub and an interactive night storyKampanjski hub i interaktivna noćna priča

After sex, people need clear paths — not shame. Nakon seksa ljudima trebaju jasni putevi – ne sram.

Play Three Nights, a short episodic story set in a nocturnal Croatia, and see why the country needs clearer routes to testing, PrEP, PEP and CheckPoint-style counseling without stigma. Zaigraj Tri noći, kratku epizodnu priču smještenu u noćnu Hrvatsku, i saznaj zašto zemlji trebaju jasnije rute do testiranja, PrEP-a, PEP-a i CheckPoint-style savjetovanja bez stigme.

Gdje gledatiWhere to look

Počni od službenog HZJZ imenika savjetovališta/testiranja, HUHIV CheckPoint Zagreb stranice i, za PEP, informacija Klinike za infektivne bolesti.Start with the official HZJZ testing/counseling directory, the HUHIV CheckPoint Zagreb page and, for PEP, infectious disease clinic information.

Što rećiWhat to say

"Imam pitanje o mogućoj izloženosti HIV-u/STI-ju. Trebam stručnu procjenu, testiranje ili informaciju o PEP/PrEP ruti.""I have a question about possible HIV/STI exposure. I need professional assessment, testing or information about the PEP/PrEP route."

Što pitatiWhat to ask

Pitaj gdje je najbliža službena ruta, trebaš li doći odmah, što vrijedi za period prozora i treba li kontrolno testiranje.Ask where the nearest official route is, whether you should go now, how the window period applies and whether follow-up testing is needed.

Što ne odgađatiWhat not to delay

Moguću potrebu za PEP-om, hitne simptome, nasilje, prisilu ili situaciju u kojoj se ne osjećaš sigurno.Possible need for PEP, urgent symptoms, violence, coercion or any situation where you do not feel safe.

The scandal is not sex. It is silence, stigma and unclear access. Skandal nije seks. Skandal su šutnja, stigma i nejasan pristup.

The campaign runs in three deliberate acts, in this order — because public support is strongest when it follows understanding. Kampanja se odvija u tri promišljena čina, ovim redom – jer je javna podrška najjača kad dolazi nakon razumijevanja.

Act I

The websiteWeb-stranica

Readable, searchable pages in Croatian and English — so the facts about testing, PrEP and PEP can be found by anyone, at any hour, through search, official sources and press. Čitljive stranice na hrvatskom i engleskom, vidljive tražilicama – da činjenice o testiranju, PrEP-u i PEP-u svatko može pronaći, u bilo koje doba, preko pretrage, službenih izvora i medija.

◆
Act II

The storyPriča

Three Nights follows Ema, Ivan, Petra and Sara through choices real people face — a broken condom at 2:47, a question about PrEP, a test that lives three hours away. Tri noći prate Emu, Ivana, Petru i Saru kroz odluke s kojima se stvarni ljudi susreću – puknuti kondom u 2:47, pitanje o PrEP-u, test udaljen tri sata vožnje.

◆
Act III

The petitionPeticija

Each finished night unlocks one concrete demand. The petition is not active yet; for now, the Night Ledger points to the campaign demands and launch TODOs. Svaka završena noć otključava jedan konkretan zahtjev. Peticija još nije aktivna; za sada Noćna bilanca vodi na zahtjeve kampanje i TODO-e prije pokretanja.

◆

A short story about a real system gap. Kratka priča o stvarnoj rupi u sustavu.

Every character and event in the story is fictional, and the game never asks for personal details. What is real is the map underneath: where the routes are clear, and where they simply stop. Svaki lik i događaj u priči izmišljen je, a igra nikada ne traži osobne podatke. Stvarna je jedino karta ispod svega: gdje su rute jasne, a gdje jednostavno prestaju.

A petition should follow understanding, not pressure. Peticija treba slijediti razumijevanje, a ne pritisak.

The story ends by translating three nights into campaign demands: a clear urgent PEP assessment route, clearer and fairer PrEP access, and CheckPoint-style community access within real reach of every region. Priča završava tako da tri noći prevodi u zahtjeve kampanje: jasnu urgentnu rutu za procjenu PEP-a, jasniji i pravedniji PrEP pristup te CheckPoint-style pristup u zajednici stvarno nadohvat svakoj regiji.
